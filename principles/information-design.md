# Information Design Principles

1. **Metrics are evidence, not the primary interface structure.** [R19]
2. **Show change, not inventory** – prioritise what changed and why. [R19]
3. **Context before precision.** [R19]
4. **Relationships are first-class objects** – model entities and links explicitly. [R19]
5. **Every statement is addressable** – link claims to data, source or primary document. [R19 StoryFacets] [R18 Epoch Lives primary sources]
6. **Separate fact from inference.** [R19]
7. **Multiple perspectives** – a dataset has no single correct chart. [R19 Data Clocks]
8. **Personal relevance** – anchor global topics in the user's own context where responsible. [R18 Glocal Climate Change] [R19 Judge my Music]
9. **Keep semantics stable** – one definition per metric/entity. [R19]
10. **Information architecture before UI** – define the objects (concepts, events, entities, insights, decisions) and their relationships before choosing layouts. [G]
