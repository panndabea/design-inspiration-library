# Comparative Split View

## Description
Two or more realities, scenarios or segments are shown in parallel with synchronised structure.

## UX goal
Make differences immediately visible.

## Visual characteristics
Aligned panels with identical scales and layout; differences highlighted.

## Interaction behavior
Choose what to compare, synchronised scrolling/hover.

## Suitable project types
- scenario comparison
- segment/region comparison
- before/after
- competing approaches

## Unsuitable project types
- more than a few parallel views (report limitation)

## Implementation considerations
Share scales and state between panels.

## Accessibility and performance
Provide a difference summary in text.

## Anti-patterns and risks
- different scales in parallel panels
